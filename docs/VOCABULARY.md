# Motion Density Vocabulary (v1)
Printable version: docs/Motion_Density_Vocabulary.pdf

**Density** — meaningful information + motion per second, without clutter. Rated D1–D5.

| Sub-term | Measures | D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 | D5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beat rate | new event every… | 4s+ | ~3s | 1.5–2s | 1–1.5s | <1s |
| Layer count | animated layers at once | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–5 | 5–7 | 7+ |
| Data load | real numbers per scene | 1 | 1–2 | 2–4 | 4–6 | 6+ |
| Motion vocabulary | distinct techniques | 3 | 5 | ~10 | 15+ | 20+ |
| 3D share | % runtime with real 3D | 0% | 0–10% | 20–40% | 40–60% | 60%+ |
| Sound sync | % beats with a sound hit | ~30% | ~50% | ~80% | ~90% | 100% |

- **Beat** — one new event (a number counting, a card landing, a word revealing).
- **Hero moment** — a big "wow" shot. 3–5 per minute.
- **Brand fidelity** — only real client assets and numbers. Nothing invented.
- **Locked camera** — the camera never orbits/tilts; only objects move. Push-ins allowed.
- **Frame-check** — stills of every beat reviewed before a full render.
- **Rung** — one step on the quality ladder (one video = one rung).
- **Hold** — a deliberate pause so the viewer can read.
- **Free audio** — copyright-free, commercial-use music/SFX only; paid licence only if the client pays.

Brief examples: "Make this rung D4: beat rate 1–1.5s, 3D share 50%." · "Data load is low in Act 2." · "Add a hero moment at the logo, keep the locked camera." · "Too dense here, add a hold."
