# DevAegis: "The Kill Switch", a 60 s vertical film (9:16, 24 fps, 1440 frames)

**Cast**
- DEV: Rocketbox Male_Adult_20 (brown hoodie).
- CLIENT: Rocketbox Business_Male_04 (older, bald, shirt and vest).

**Sets**
- DEV ROOM: night, a warm desk lamp against a cold city window.
- CLIENT OFFICE: dusk, glass, a skyline behind.

**Look**
- Teal/amber contrast.
- Shallow depth of field on close-ups; handheld micro-drift only on tense beats.
- Captions are one word, in DevAegis amber (Plus Jakarta Sans 800).
- Screens carry the story and are always held long enough to read (at least 2.5 s).
